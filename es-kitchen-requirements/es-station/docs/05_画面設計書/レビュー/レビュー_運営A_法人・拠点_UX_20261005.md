## Notes
- Not reviewed in detail: Q/S message wording of all IDs, and the vi text.
- AW_PLAN/DISC/SURV use the P-FORMBOX banner (保存できません); AW_CORP/BRCH do not (#5). 項目を検索 / すべて閉じる / カード目次 exist only in CORP/BRCH.
- Good: status badges always carry text; destructive modals use verb buttons; Q02 is defined for cancel, navigation and browser close; the empty/0 state, the permission-denied CSV direct open, the E31 overwrite modal and the load-fail states in detail tables are covered.
